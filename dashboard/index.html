How the value is calculated

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × age multiplier

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap/TVL of live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 comparables), otherwise the global comparable median. Measured from this snapshot, clamped to 0.05–1.50. A DEX is not priced like a lending market.
  • Growth multiplier — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) × 0.5. Momentum counts, but a single wild week cannot more than 1.5× a protocol.
  • Age multiplier — 0.80 + 0.20 × min(age, 730 days) / 730. A protocol that has run two years without a token is worth a little more than a two-week-old one; age is a weak signal, category and growth do the work.

What is excluded, and why

Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are never ranked: WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum, Binance staked ETH is ETH in a staking product, and neither is a protocol still waiting on a launch of its own. Versioned pools (V2, 2.0) and exchange products are out for the same reason. The board is the watchlist's 40 protocols plus every other protocol the same rule keeps — 198 rows in all.

Implied values are this radar's own claim, never make.fun's. A protocol is described as one that records no token, never as one that has none: its DefiLlama record may simply not carry it.
